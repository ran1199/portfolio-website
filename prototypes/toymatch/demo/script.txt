## Title
This is ToyMatch, an iPhone shopping app that helps people choose a toy as a gift.
## Introduction
The shopper is a relative or family friend buying a birthday gift for a child aged three to twelve.
They have a budget and a date, but they don't know much about what the child likes or already owns.
Today, they jump between product pages and reviews, and age labels don't say whether a toy really fits.
We estimate that takes about an hour per gift, and ToyMatch aims to cut it to about twenty minutes.
I'll walk you through one birthday gift, from the first question to the store.
## Gift details
The shopper opens ToyMatch and taps Find a gift.
The child is turning eight, the budget is fifty dollars, and the party is in two weeks.
They know the child likes science and building, and already has building sets.
If they don't know something, they tap Not sure, and ToyMatch won't guess.
## Shortlist
ToyMatch removes every toy that misses the age, budget or delivery date, then ranks the rest by interests and reviews.
Each card shows the price, age range, rating, and a short reason it may fit.
If nothing feels right, the refresh button shows other toys that still fit.
## Compare
The shopper picks two favorites and taps Compare.
Price, age fit, strengths, drawbacks, assembly, batteries and warnings line up side by side.
## Details
Opening the terrarium kit shows why it may fit, what buyers praise and complain about, and the sources.
It also flags what to know before buying, like batteries that aren't included.
This is the moment of value: in a few minutes, the shopper knows why this toy fits this child.
That's the difference from searching Amazon.
Amazon shows long lists of results and star ratings, but not whether a toy fits this child, budget and date.
ToyMatch does that work first, and the shopper can still buy at Amazon.
## Where to buy
Next, the shopper chooses where to buy.
Amazon delivers fastest, and Target is a few dollars cheaper.
They choose Target, tap Buy, and ToyMatch hands them off to the store to check out.
## Customer concern
Expect customers to ask: why should I trust these picks?
Point to the screen: every pick shows its reasons, review sources and drawbacks, and anything uncertain is marked.
The shopper can check the reasoning instead of taking it on faith.
## Closing
To recap: people buying for a child they don't know well lose time and confidence.
ToyMatch gives them a short list that fits, explains why, and sends them to the store they choose.
